CoroRacing Studio

CoroRacing Studio es la herramienta interna con la que el propio canal prepara sus videos y los sube a su propio canal de YouTube mediante la API de datos de YouTube. No es un servicio público: no tiene usuarios externos ni registro, y solo la usa el titular del canal.

Usa los servicios de la API de YouTube. Al usarla, aceptas los Términos de servicio de YouTube.

About (English)

CoroRacing is a Spanish-language YouTube channel with short videos about motorsport history, technology and legendary moments (Formula 1, MotoGP, Dakar, Le Mans and more).

CoroRacing Studio is the channel's private, internal tool. It is used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, and to post one comment from the channel on those same videos. It has no external users, no sign-up and is not offered to the public.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
